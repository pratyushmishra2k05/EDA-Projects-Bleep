# 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Portfolio

Welcome to my **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Portfolio**! This repository showcases two data analytics projects focused on discovering patterns, analyzing trends, and extracting meaningful insights from real-world datasets using Python.

## 🚀 Projects Overview

### 01. E-Commerce Sales & Customer Behavior Analysis

**Objective:** Analyze online retail transactions to understand sales performance, product demand, and customer purchasing behavior.

**Dataset:** [UCI Online Retail Dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online+retail)

**Key Analysis Areas:**
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Sales and revenue analysis
- Top-selling products and product performance
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Country-wise sales analysis
- Identification of trends and business insights

**Technologies:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

**Project Folder:** `01-Ecommerce-Sales/`

---

### 02. Bank Marketing Campaign Analysis

**Objective:** Explore customer demographics, marketing campaign characteristics, and factors associated with term-deposit subscriptions.

**Dataset:** [UCI Bank Marketing Dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/222/bank+marketing)

**Key Analysis Areas:**
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Customer demographic analysis
- Campaign contact and outcome analysis
- Subscription distribution
- Analysis of previous campaign outcomes
- Identification of patterns associated with successful subscriptions

**Technologies:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

**Project Folder:** `02-Bank-Marketing/`

---
