# E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## Overview
This project explores transaction data from an online retailer to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product revenue and monthly trends.

## Dataset
Source: [UCI Online Retail Dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online+retail)

## Tools
- Python
- Pandas and NumPy
- Matplotlib and Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis
- Data inspection and cleaning
- Product and country revenue comparisons
- Monthly sales trends
- Customer spending analysis
- Quantity, price and revenue relationships
- Cancellation analysis

## Key results
- Original records: 541,909
- Records in the completed-sales dataset: 392,692
- Revenue in the completed-sales dataset: £8,887,208.89
- Unique customers in the customer-linked dataset: 4,338

## Files
- `ecommerce_sales_analysis.ipynb`: analysis and visualizations
- `Online Retail.xlsx`: original dataset

## Notes
Revenue figures are calculated after the cleaning steps documented in the notebook. The analysis excludes cancellations and non-positive quantities or prices from the completed-sales dataset.
